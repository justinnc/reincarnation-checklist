# Gate 1 step 2 — Real-user session kit (EN + VI)

> Ready-to-run script for the owner. It covers the three things `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` Gate 1 step 2 asks for: the participant **explains a return**, **finds the source**, and **completes a second-surface journey**. Nothing here is user evidence until sessions are actually run; the only existing review is simulated (`gate1-simulated-persona-feedback.md`).

## Setup

- **Who:** people from both audiences, at least one phone and one laptop per language. A suggested minimum is 3 English and 3 Vietnamese sessions; that number is a rule of thumb, not a requirement from the plan.
- **Length:** 15–20 minutes each, one participant at a time, on their own device.
- **Site:** https://reincarnation-checklist-justinncs-projects.vercel.app (`/en` or `/vi`).
- **Your role:** watch and take notes. Do not explain the site, point at the screen or answer "is this right?" until the end. If they are stuck for a minute, note it and say "what would you try next?".

## Privacy (keep it this way)

- Ask before starting: "I'm testing the website, not you. I'll take anonymous notes, no recording. OK?" / "Mình đang thử trang web chứ không kiểm tra bạn. Mình chỉ ghi chú ẩn danh, không ghi âm ghi hình. Bạn đồng ý chứ?"
- Label notes **EN-1, EN-2, VI-1…** only. Do not write names, contacts, employers, ages, photos or screen recordings into the repo or any shared file.
- Record only a broad device type (phone / laptop) and whether they have invested before (yes / no).
- If someone asks to withdraw, delete their sheet.

## Script

Read each task aloud, then stay quiet. Vietnamese wording is given for `/vi` sessions.

| # | Task (EN) | Nhiệm vụ (VI) | What you are checking |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | "Take 30 seconds. What is this site for?" | "Bạn xem 30 giây. Trang này để làm gì?" | Purpose understood without help |
| 1 | "Pick any opportunity on the home page that looks interesting and open it." | "Chọn một cơ hội bất kỳ ở trang chủ mà bạn thấy thú vị rồi mở ra." | Can reach a milestone detail page |
| 2 | "In your own words: what would have happened to 1,000 put in, and how is that number worked out?" | "Theo cách hiểu của bạn: bỏ vào 1.000 thì thành bao nhiêu, và con số đó được tính thế nào?" | **Explains a return**: entry price, exit price, date, splits; notices it is historical |
| 3 | "Where does this number come from? Show me how you'd check it." | "Con số này lấy từ đâu? Bạn thử chỉ cách kiểm tra lại." | **Finds the source** link; notices the "not independently fact-checked" line |
| 4 | "Is this site telling you to buy this?" | "Trang này có đang khuyên bạn mua không?" | Reads it as history/entertainment, not advice |
| 5 | "Now play the Survival game once, start to finish." | "Bây giờ bạn chơi Thử thách sống sót một lượt từ đầu đến cuối." | **Second-surface journey**: can start, understands the three choices and the 3%/year rule on Skip, reaches the pass card |
| 6 | "What did the result screen tell you?" | "Màn hình kết quả cho bạn biết điều gì?" | Understands rank, net worth and what was real history |
| 7 | "You think one fact on the site is wrong. What would you do?" | "Giả sử bạn thấy một thông tin trên trang bị sai. Bạn sẽ làm gì?" | Finds Methodology → corrections; note if "needs a GitHub account" stops them |
| 8 | "Anything confusing, annoying or that you didn't trust?" | "Có gì khó hiểu, khó chịu, hoặc khiến bạn không tin không?" | Open feedback |

If time allows, swap task 5 for the Quiz, the Price Almanac or the What-If playbook on alternate sessions so each surface is seen at least once.

## After the sessions

1. Tally each task across sessions (for example "Task 3: 4 of 6 alone"). Report counts only; with this few sessions, do not turn them into percentages or conversion rates.
2. Rate each problem: **blocker** (could not finish a task), **misleading** (finished but believed something false), **friction** (slow or annoyed), **cosmetic**.
3. Anything rated *misleading* about a number or a source goes to the claim ledger (`docs/research/claim-audit-ledger.md`) before any data edit.
4. Save the anonymized summary as `docs/reviews/gate1-real-user-sessions.md`. That file, not this kit, is what closes Gate 1 step 2.
